# Getting help

- **Questions:** [open an issue](https://github.com/sroberts/outpost/issues/new/choose)
  and say it's a question; it will be labelled `question`.
- **Bugs:** [open an issue](https://github.com/sroberts/outpost/issues/new/choose)
  with the bug report form. `outpost version` and `outpost status <service>`
  output make most reports answerable on the first reply.
- **Security issues:** see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Outpost is maintained by one person in spare time, so replies may take a few
days.
