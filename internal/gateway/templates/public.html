How Funnel works

What's reachable

Only an app's public paths are reachable from the internet, at the app's own address; nothing else of the app, and nothing of the portal. The app still has to check each webhook's signature.

Funnel needs the funnel attribute for the app's node in your tailnet policy, and HTTPS turned on for the tailnet. Without them it shows as unavailable, with Tailscale's reason.
