Internet access

{{with .Public}}

An app's public paths skip the login. With Funnel on, Tailscale also answers them from the public internet, at the app's own address, for webhooks from GitHub, Stripe and the like. Nothing else of the app, and nothing of the portal, is reachable that way. The app still has to check each webhook's signature. What you set here wins over the app's manifest.
