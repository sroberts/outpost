How it works

Routing, feeds, sending

  • Every minute Outpost looks at new mail in the inbox. Mail sent to an app's name or one of its random addresses is kept for that app, labelled Outpost/<app> and archived. Mail to any other address is never opened or moved.

  • Each address has an Atom feed, so a feed reader such as Yarr can subscribe to the newsletters sent to it.

  • Apps read their mail, and send it if allowed, at OUTPOST_MAIL_URL with their own OUTPOST_MAIL_TOKEN. They never see the password.
